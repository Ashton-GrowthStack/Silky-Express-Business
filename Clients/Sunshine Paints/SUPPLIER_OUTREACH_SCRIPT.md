## 🔧 TECHNICAL QUESTIONS TO ASK

### **CRITICAL SPECIFICATIONS:**

**1. Capacity & Throughput:**
- Q: "What's the maximum batch capacity (liters)?"
- Q: "How many color matches per hour can you handle?"
- Q: "What's the maximum daily throughput?"

**2. Accuracy & Precision:**
- Q: "What's the dispensing accuracy (±ml)?"
- Q: "Do you have ±0.1ml precision capability?"
- Q: "How consistent are results color-to-color?"

**3. Paint Compatibility:**
- Q: "Does it work with water-based paints?"
- Q: "Does it work with oil-based/solvent paints?"
- Q: "What paint viscosity range do you support?"
- Q: "Can it handle metallic/pearlescent paints?"

**4. Color Input System:**
- Q: "What color input methods do you offer?"
  - Barcode scanning?
  - Digital keypad entry?
  - Computer software input?
  - Color chart selection?
- Q: "How many colors can your database store?"
- Q: "Can we input custom color formulas?"

**5. Automation Level:**
- Q: "For semi-auto: What does operator do?"
- Q: "For full-auto: Is it completely hands-off?"
- Q: "How intuitive is the user interface?"

**6. Reliability & Maintenance:**
- Q: "What's the expected uptime %?"
- Q: "What maintenance is required? (frequency/cost)"
- Q: "Self-cleaning capability?"
- Q: "Warranty period & what's covered?"

**7. Software & Support:**
- Q: "Is software included in price?"
- Q: "Can you update color database remotely?"
- Q: "What training is provided?"
- Q: "Technical support availability?"

**8. Delivery & Implementation:**
- Q: "Lead time from order to delivery?"
- Q: "Can you install in South Africa or nearby?"
- Q: "What's included in installation?"
- Q: "Installation timeline?"
- Q: "Are spare parts available locally?"

**9. ROI & Economics:**
- Q: "Average cost per color match?"
- Q: "What's the expected ROI timeline?"
- Q: "Electricity consumption?"
- Q: "Operating costs (consumables, maintenance)?"

**10. References:**
- Q: "Can you provide 2-3 references?"
- Q: "Any installations in Africa/South Africa?"
- Q: "Any installations in automotive retail?"

## 💬 CONVERSATION FLOW STRATEGY

### **PHASE 1: Initial Contact**
- Send professional message
- Ask for catalog & basic specs
- Mention you're evaluating multiple options
- Give 3-5 day response deadline

### **PHASE 2: Technical Deep Dive**
- Review their response
- Ask detailed technical questions
- Request references
- Ask about South Africa support

### **PHASE 3: Quotation**
- Request formal written quote
- Confirm all costs included
- Clarify payment terms
- Get timeline in writing

### **PHASE 4: Reference Verification**
- Call their references
- Ask about reliability, support, value
- Ask about South Africa experience
- Ask if they'd recommend

### **PHASE 5: Negotiation**
- If close, try to negotiate
- Ask for bundled pricing
- Request extended warranty
- Discuss payment terms

### **PHASE 6: Decision**
- Score all suppliers
- Compare total cost of ownership
- Make final selection
- Get everything in writing
