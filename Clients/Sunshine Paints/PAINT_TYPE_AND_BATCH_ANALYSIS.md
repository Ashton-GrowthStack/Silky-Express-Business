## 🎯 WHY BATCH SIZE MATTERS

### **Different Machines for Different Batch Sizes:**

**Small Batch Machine** (0.5-2L batches)
- Good for: Multiple small jobs per day
- Capacity: 1-5 liters per batch
- Speed: ~30 seconds per batch
- Best for: Retail paint stores (like Sunshine Paints)

**Medium Batch Machine** (2-5L batches)
- Good for: Standard auto body shop volumes
- Capacity: 5-10 liters per batch
- Speed: ~60-90 seconds per batch
- Best for: High-volume retail or light production

**Large Batch Machine** (10-20L batches)
- Good for: Production shops, paint manufacturers
- Capacity: 20+ liters per batch
- Speed: 2-5 minutes per batch
- Not needed for Sunshine Paints

### **Our Estimate = Small-to-Medium Batch Machine**

Based on 1.5-2.5L average + 35-50 daily mixes:
- **Recommend:** Small-to-medium capacity machine
- **Throughput:** 20-40 mixes per hour
- **Batch size:** 1-3 liters comfortably
- **Best suppliers:** Foshan Ego Tinting (designed for retail paint shops)
