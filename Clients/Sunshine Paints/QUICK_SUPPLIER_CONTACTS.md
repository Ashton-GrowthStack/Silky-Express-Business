## 🔧 MUST-ASK QUESTIONS (After They Send Specs)

**Copy & send to supplier:**

```
Thank you for the information. I have some technical questions:

1. What's the dispensing accuracy/tolerance? (±0.1ml?)
2. How many color matches per hour?
3. Does it support barcode color input?
4. Can it handle both water-based and oil-based automotive paint?
5. What's included - software, color database, training?
6. What's the exact price breakdown (equipment, software, installation, shipping to Durban)?
7. Lead time from order to installation?
8. Can you provide 2-3 references (preferably from Africa)?
9. What's your after-sales support for South Africa?
10. Warranty period & what's covered?

Please also send:
- Full technical specifications sheet
- Payment terms options
- Formal quote

Thanks
```

- ✅ "We need ±0.1ml precision dispensing"
- ✅ "Color code input capability (barcode or digital)"
- ✅ "We handle both water-based and oil-based automotive paint"
- ✅ "Daily volume is 20-50+ custom color matches"
- ✅ "Looking at semi-automatic vs fully automatic options"
- ✅ "We need reliable after-sales support in South Africa"
- ✅ "Can you provide references from similar installations?"
- ✅ "What's the ROI timeline and cost per color match?"
- ✅ "We're evaluating multiple suppliers"
- ✅ "Lead time and installation timeline critical"

## 🚫 DON'T SAY THESE (Sounds Unprofessional)

- ❌ "I don't know much about paint machines"
- ❌ "My boss told me to get quotes"
- ❌ "What's the cheapest option?"

- ❌ "When can you deliver?" (without context)
