## **PHASE 4: EVALUATION & FOLLOW-UP (Days 4-7)**

### **STEP 5: Evaluate Responses & Ask Follow-Up Questions**

**Action:** Review each response carefully using evaluation criteria

**When Response Arrives:**

**Checklist for Each Response:**

- [ ] Did they provide product specs? (Y/N)
- [ ] Did they provide pricing? (Y/N)
- [ ] Did they mention DDP shipping? (Y/N)
- [ ] Did they provide lead time? (Y/N)
- [ ] Did they mention South Africa experience? (Y/N)
- [ ] Response time: _____ hours (track this)
- [ ] Professional communication? (Y/N)
- [ ] Any red flags? (List them)

**Green Flags to Look For:**
- ✅ Quick response (24-48 hours)
- ✅ Detailed specifications
- ✅ Clear pricing breakdown
- ✅ Mentions South Africa/Africa experience
- ✅ Professional communication
- ✅ Offers DDP terms
- ✅ Provides references

**Red Flags to Watch:**
- ❌ Slow response (>3 days)
- ❌ Vague specs ("high accuracy")
- ❌ Hidden costs
- ❌ Won't provide references
- ❌ No South Africa experience mentioned
- ❌ Pushy sales tactics
- ❌ Poor English/typos

---

### **STEP 6: Send Technical Follow-Up Questions**

**Action:** Send detailed technical questions to all responsive suppliers

**Use from QUICK_SUPPLIER_CONTACTS.md - "Follow-Up Questions" section**

**Message:**

```
Thank you for the information. I have some technical questions:

1. What's the dispensing accuracy/tolerance? (±0.1ml?)
2. How many color matches per hour?
3. Does it support barcode color input?
4. Can it handle both water-based and oil-based automotive paint?
5. What's included - software, color database, training?
6. What's the exact price breakdown (equipment, software, installation, shipping to Durban)?
7. Lead time from order to installation?
8. Can you provide 2-3 references (preferably from Africa)?
9. What's your after-sales support for South Africa?
10. Warranty period & what's covered?

Please also send:
- Full technical specifications sheet
- Payment terms options
- Formal quote

Thanks
```

**Send to:** All suppliers that responded with basic info

**Expected Response Time:** 48-72 hours for detailed responses

### **STEP 8: Score Each Supplier (1-10 Scale)**

**Action:** Rate each supplier on multiple criteria

**Scoring Criteria:**

```
SCORING SYSTEM (0-10 per category):
10 = Excellent, exactly what we need
8-9 = Very good, meets requirements
6-7 = Good, acceptable
4-5 = Adequate, some concerns
0-3 = Poor, missing critical requirements

CATEGORIES TO SCORE:
1. Product Quality/Accuracy (25% weight) → Score × 0.25
2. Price Competitiveness (20% weight) → Score × 0.20
3. Reliability/Track Record (20% weight) → Score × 0.20
4. Communication/Responsiveness (15% weight) → Score × 0.15
5. South Africa Support/Experience (20% weight) → Score × 0.20

TOTAL SCORE = Sum of weighted scores (Max 100)
```

**Example:**

```
FOSHAN EGO TINTING SCORING:
Quality/Accuracy: 9/10 × 0.25 = 2.25
Price: 8/10 × 0.20 = 1.60
Reliability: 9/10 × 0.20 = 1.80
Communication: 9/10 × 0.15 = 1.35
SA Support: 8/10 × 0.20 = 1.60
───────────────────────────────
TOTAL SCORE = 8.6/10 (86%)
```
