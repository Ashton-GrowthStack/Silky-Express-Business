# Sunshine Paints - Daily Paint Volume Calculation

**Purpose:** Answer supplier's question: "How many KG per day?"  
**Date:** August 21, 2026

---

## 📊 CALCULATION FORMULA

**Daily Paint Volume (KG) = Number of Daily Mixes × Average Paint Size per Mix**

---

## 🎯 SCENARIO ANALYSIS

### **SCENARIO 1: CONSERVATIVE ESTIMATE** (Lower Volume)

**Assumptions:**
- Daily color matches: 20 mixes
- Average paint per mix: 1.5 liters (small cans)
- Paint density: ~1.4 kg/liter (automotive paint)

**Calculation:**
- 20 mixes × 1.5 liters = 30 liters per day
- 30 liters × 1.4 kg/liter = **42 KG per day**

---

### **SCENARIO 2: MODERATE ESTIMATE** (Typical Volume) ⭐ **MOST LIKELY**

**Assumptions:**
- Daily color matches: 35 mixes
- Average paint per mix: 2 liters (standard cans)
- Paint density: ~1.4 kg/liter

**Calculation:**
- 35 mixes × 2 liters = 70 liters per day
- 70 liters × 1.4 kg/liter = **98 KG per day (~100 KG)**

---

### **SCENARIO 3: HIGH VOLUME ESTIMATE** (Peak Days)

**Assumptions:**
- Daily color matches: 50 mixes
- Average paint per mix: 2.5 liters (larger cans)
- Paint density: ~1.4 kg/liter

**Calculation:**
- 50 mixes × 2.5 liters = 125 liters per day
- 125 liters × 1.4 kg/liter = **175 KG per day**

## 💡 WHAT THIS MEANS FOR MACHINE SELECTION

### **For 100 KG/day (Moderate Volume):**

**Semi-Automatic Machine:**
- Capacity needed: 20-30 liters per hour
- Dispensing speed: ~0.5-1 liter per minute
- Suitable for: 35-50 mixes per day

**Fully Automatic Machine:**
- Capacity needed: 30-50 liters per hour
- Dispensing speed: ~1-2 liters per minute
- Suitable for: 50+ mixes per day
