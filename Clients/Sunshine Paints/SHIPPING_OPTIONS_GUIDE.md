## 🚢 THREE SHIPPING OPTIONS EXPLAINED

### **OPTION 1: FOB (Free on Board)** ❌ NOT RECOMMENDED for first-time import

**Who pays what:**
- **Supplier:** Covers production + packing + loading onto ship at China port
- **YOU (Buyer):** Pay for everything after ship loading
  - Ocean freight
  - Insurance
  - South Africa customs clearance
  - Duties & taxes (30% + anti-dumping + levies + VAT)
  - Local delivery to warehouse

**Timeline:**
- Lead time: 3-4 weeks (port to port)
- Customs clearance: 2-5 days (or longer if issues)
- Total: 4-5 weeks

**Cost Example (20ft container with paint mixer):**
```
Equipment FOB Price:          $12,000
Ocean Freight:                $2,500
Insurance:                    $300
Customs Duties (30%):         $4,230
Environment Levy:             $3,400
Anti-dumping (est 10%):       $1,530
VAT (15%):                    $3,826
Customs Broker Fee:           R3,000 (~$200)
Local Delivery:               $800
─────────────────────────────
TOTAL LANDED COST:            ~$28,386

Cost per unit: $28,386
```

**Advantages:**
- ✅ Lower equipment price (supplier doesn't handle export)
- ✅ You have control over logistics
- ✅ Can use your own freight forwarder

**Disadvantages:**
- ❌ Complex process (need customs broker knowledge)
- ❌ High risk if duties/taxes higher than estimated
- ❌ Difficult for first-time importers
- ❌ Need South African customs code (SARS registration)
- ❌ Multiple parties = more coordination
- ❌ Cash flow risk (can't predict exact duties upfront)

---

### **OPTION 2: CIF (Cost, Insurance, Freight)** ⚠️ MODERATE - For experienced importers

**Who pays what:**
- **Supplier:** Covers production + packing + ocean freight + insurance to Durban port
- **YOU (Buyer):** Pay customs clearance onwards
  - South Africa customs broker
  - Duties & taxes (30% + anti-dumping + levies + VAT)
  - Local delivery to warehouse

**Timeline:**
- Lead time: 3-4 weeks (door to port)
- Customs clearance: 2-5 days
- Local delivery: 1-2 days
- Total: 4-5 weeks

**Cost Example:**
```
Equipment CIF Price:          $14,500 (includes freight + insurance)
Customs Duties (30%):         $4,350
Environment Levy:             $3,400
Anti-dumping (est 10%):       $1,590
VAT (15%):                    $3,911
Customs Broker Fee:           R3,500 (~$230)
Local Delivery:               $800
─────────────────────────────
TOTAL LANDED COST:            ~$28,781

Cost per unit: $28,781
```

**Advantages:**
- ✅ Supplier handles export/freight
- ✅ More transparent freight cost
- ✅ Moderate price
- ✅ Some control over local delivery

**Disadvantages:**
- ⚠️ Still need customs broker in South Africa
- ⚠️ You handle duties & taxes (need SARS code)
- ⚠️ Cash flow risk from duty surprises
- ⚠️ Less total control than FOB
- ⚠️ Still complex process

---

### **OPTION 3: DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)** ✅ RECOMMENDED for Sunshine Paints

**Who pays what:**
- **Supplier:** Covers EVERYTHING
  - Production
  - Packing
  - Inland transport to port (China)
  - Export customs clearance (China)
  - Ocean freight
  - Insurance
  - South Africa customs clearance
  - Duties & taxes
  - Local delivery to your warehouse
- **YOU (Buyer):** Just receive the machine, ready to use

**Timeline:**
- Lead time: 4-5 weeks (all-in door-to-door)
- Everything handled by supplier
- Total: Single, predictable timeline

**Cost Example:**
```
Equipment DDP Price (All-in):  $22,000

That's it! Everything included to your door.
```

**Advantages:**
- ✅ SIMPLEST - One supplier, one price, one invoice
- ✅ No customs broker needed (supplier handles)
- ✅ No SARS registration needed
- ✅ No duty surprises (all-in pricing)
- ✅ Best for first-time importers
- ✅ Supplier accountable for entire delivery
- ✅ Easier cash flow planning
- ✅ Machine arrives ready to install
- ✅ Single point of contact
- ✅ No need to understand South African customs

**Disadvantages:**
- ❌ Higher equipment price (supplier absorbs risk + duties)
- ❌ Less control over logistics process
- ❌ Can't choose your own freight forwarder
- ❌ Longer lead time potentially
