## 2.9 KNOWN PROBLEMS WITH TWO-COMPONENT POLYURETHANE SYSTEMS

### 🚨 Application Challenges:

**Mixing Errors → Cracking**
- Requires weight-based measurement (NOT volume)
- Pot life: 2-30 minutes (VERY FAST)
- Labor intensive: Minimum 5-person crews
- One mistake ruins entire batch

**Environmental Sensitivity:**
- Temperature >30°C = shorter pot life
- Must have substrate moisture <75% RH
- Requires well-ventilated area
- Moisture contamination = failed application

**Common Durability Issues:**
- ❌ Cracking from substrate movement
- ❌ Air bubbles/voids from bad application
- ❌ Tacky surfaces from wrong mixing ratios
- ✅ Once cured properly: More durable than epoxy alternatives
