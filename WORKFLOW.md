# Complete Supplier Workflow - Step by Step Guide

**Purpose:** End-to-end guide for finding, evaluating, and selecting suppliers  
**Duration:** Typically 2-4 weeks per client project  
**Outcome:** Selected & approved supplier → Permanent entry in Suppliers folder

---

## 🔄 The 7-Step Workflow

```
START
  ↓
[STEP 1] Client Intake
  ↓
[STEP 2] Research & Discovery
  ↓
[STEP 3] Build Comparison
  ↓
[STEP 4] Client Feedback Loop
  ↓
[STEP 5] Narrow Down Options
  ↓
[STEP 6] Final Recommendation & Approval
  ↓
[STEP 7] Archive to Suppliers Folder
  ↓
END (Ready for Orders)
```

---

## STEP 1: CLIENT INTAKE

**⏱ Duration:** 1-2 days  
**📍 Location:** `/Clients/[New Client Name]/`

### What to Document:

Create **`Client_Brief.md`**:

```markdown
# [Client Name] - Project Brief

**Date:** [Today]
**Client Company:** [Full name]
**Contact Person:** [Name, email, phone]
**Project Status:** 🔄 In Progress

## What They Need

**Product Category:** [e.g., Decorative paving materials]
**Specific Product:** [e.g., Resin-bound aggregate]
**Quantity Needed:** [Amount/timeline]
**Target Locations:** [Geography]

## Budget & Timeline

**Budget:** USD $[Amount] or approx. cost per unit
**Timeline:** Need by [Date]
**Flexibility:** [Any flexibility on budget/timeline?]

## Quality Requirements

- [ ] Requirement 1: [Spec]
- [ ] Requirement 2: [Spec]
- [ ] Requirement 3: [Spec]

## Special Considerations

- [Any certifications needed?]
- [Climate/environmental factors?]
- [Compliance requirements?]

## Notes

[Any other important info from initial conversation]
```

### Questions to Ask Client:

- ✅ Exactly what product/service do you need?
- ✅ What's your budget per unit/total?
- ✅ When do you need it by?
- ✅ What quality standards matter most?
- ✅ Any specific certifications required?
- ✅ Geographic/climate considerations?
- ✅ Previous suppliers? Why switching?
- ✅ Decision-making process/timeline?

---

## STEP 2: RESEARCH & DISCOVERY

**⏱ Duration:** 3-5 days  
**📍 Location:** `/Clients/[Client Name]/`  
**Output:** `Supplier_Research.md`

### Research Sources:

- 🔍 Google search for industry leaders
- 🔍 Industry directories & databases
- 🔍 Trade associations & certification bodies
- 🔍 Previous projects & supplier references
- 🔍 LinkedIn & company research
- 🔍 Customer reviews & testimonials
- 🔍 Ask client about known suppliers

### Create **`Supplier_Research.md`**:

```markdown
# Supplier Research for [Product]

**Date:** [Today]
**Product Category:** [What we're sourcing]

## Potential Suppliers Identified

### Supplier A
- **Company Name:** [Full name]
- **Location:** [City, Country]
- **Years in Business:** [X years]
- **Key Products:** [What they make]
- **Estimated Price Range:** $X-Y per unit
- **Lead Time:** [Approx days/weeks]
- **Certifications:** [If known]
- **References:** [Any known clients?]
- **Source Found:** [Where you found them]
- **Contact Info:** [Email/phone if available]
- **Initial Assessment:** [First impressions - good, concerning, needs more info]

### Supplier B
[Same format]

### Supplier C
[Same format]

## Initial Shortlist

**Recommended for deeper evaluation:**
1. [Supplier A] - [Why]
2. [Supplier B] - [Why]
3. [Supplier C] - [Why]

## Notes & Next Steps

- [ ] Request detailed pricing from Suppliers A, B, C
- [ ] Ask for technical specifications
- [ ] Request references
- [ ] Request product samples (if applicable)
- [ ] Follow up with [dates]
```

### What to Request from Each Supplier:

Prepare inquiry email with:
1. Your requirements (from Client_Brief)
2. Quantity needed
3. Delivery timeline
4. Quality standards
5. Request for: pricing, samples, references, technical specs
6. Clear deadline for response

## STEP 3: BUILD COMPARISON MATRIX

**⏱ Duration:** 2-3 days  
**📍 Location:** `/Clients/[Client Name]/`  
**Output:** `Supplier_Comparison_Matrix.md`

### Create **`Supplier_Comparison_Matrix.md`**:

```markdown
# Supplier Evaluation Matrix

**Date:** [Today]  
**Product:** [What we're comparing]  
**Client:** [Client name]

| Evaluation Criteria | Weight | Supplier A | Supplier B | Supplier C |

| Certifications | 15% | ISO | ISO + ETAG | ISO |
| Product testing/data | 10% | Yes | Yes | No |
| Warranty/guarantee | 5% | 2 years | 5 years | 1 year |
| Quality references | 10% | 2 refs | 4 refs | No refs |
| **COMMERCIAL TERMS** |
| Unit price | 15% | $50 | $55 | $40 |
| MOQ (Min Order Qty) | 5% | 100 | 100 | 500 |
| Lead time | 10% | 30 days | 21 days | 45 days |
| Payment terms | 5% | 50/50 | 30/70 | Upfront |
| Shipping included | 3% | No | Yes | No |
| **RELIABILITY** |
| Years in business | 5% | 10 years | 25 years | 3 years |
| Communication | 5% | Good | Excellent | Slow |
| Financial stability | 3% | Stable | Strong | Unknown |

## Scoring

**Supplier A Total Score:** XX/100  
**Supplier B Total Score:** XX/100  
**Supplier C Total Score:** XX/100

## Qualitative Assessment

**Supplier A Pros:**
- [Advantage 1]
- [Advantage 2]

**Supplier A Cons:**
- [Disadvantage 1]
- [Disadvantage 2]

**Supplier B Pros:**
- [Advantage 1]

**Supplier B Cons:**
- [Disadvantage 1]

- [Advantage 1]

- [Disadvantage 1]
```

## STEP 4: CLIENT FEEDBACK LOOP

**⏱ Duration:** 3-7 days (iterative)  
**📍 Location:** `/Clients/[Client Name]/`  
**Output:** `Client_Feedback_Notes.md`

### Gather Client Input:

Send client comparison matrix + ask:
- Which suppliers look best to you?
- Any concerns with our recommendations?
- Any that you'd like to eliminate?
- What's most important to you? (price? quality? speed?)
- Want us to get more info on any suppliers?

### Create **`Client_Feedback_Notes.md`**:

```markdown
# Client Feedback Log

## Round 1 - Initial Reaction

**Date:** [Date]  
**From:** [Client contact name]  
**Method:** [Email/call/meeting]

**Client Comments:**
- Preferred Supplier B
- Budget concern with Supplier A (too expensive)
- Need clarification on lead time for Supplier C
- Quality is priority over price

**Action Items Identified:**
- [ ] Get more info on Supplier B's warranty
- [ ] Confirm Supplier C's lead time in writing
- [ ] Provide references for Supplier B

---

## Round 2 - After Additional Info

**Date:** [Date]  
**From:** [Client contact name]

**Client Comments:**
- Ready to decide on Supplier B
- Concerned about payment terms (prefer 30/70)
- Can we negotiate lead time?

**Action Items:**
- [ ] Contact Supplier B for payment term negotiation
- [ ] Confirm if lead time can be expedited

---

## Round 3 - Final Decision

**Date:** [Date]  
**From:** [Client contact name]

**Client Decision:** ✅ **SELECT SUPPLIER B**

**Reasoning:**
- Best quality + warranty
- Acceptable price point
- Can meet timeline
- Strong references

**Conditions:**
- Confirm 30/70 payment terms
- Get written guarantee on lead time

## Decision Summary

**APPROVED SUPPLIER:** [Supplier Name]  
**Approval Date:** [Date]  
**Approved By:** [Client contact]  
**Email Confirmation:** [Link or saved]
```

### If Negotiation Needed:

**Contact supplier with:**
- Request: "Can you adjust [payment terms/lead time/price]?"
- Reason: "Our client needs..."
- Timeline: "Decision needed by [date]"
- Alternatives: "If not, are there other options?"

---

## STEP 5: NARROW DOWN OPTIONS

**⏱ Duration:** 1-3 days  
**📍 Location:** `/Clients/[Client Name]/`

### Based on Client Feedback:

- Eliminate unsuitable options
- Refine shortlist to top 1-2 candidates
- If needed, get additional information from remaining suppliers
- Verify all key terms in writing

### Update Comparison Matrix:

Cross out eliminated suppliers, highlight chosen one

---

## STEP 7: ARCHIVE TO SUPPLIERS FOLDER

**⏱ Duration:** 1 day  
**📍 Location:** `/Suppliers/[Supplier Name]/`

### Once Client Approves Supplier:

1. **Create permanent folder:**
   ```bash
   mkdir "/Users/ashton/projects/Silky Express Business/Suppliers/[Supplier Name]"
   ```

2. **Copy all documentation from client project**
   - README template
   - All research notes
   - Comparison data
   - Client feedback
   - Final recommendation

3. **Add permanent documentation:**
   - Final contracts/agreements
   - Pricing confirmation
   - Certifications & compliance docs
   - Technical data sheets
   - Terms & conditions
   - Warranty details

4. **Create supplier README:**
   ```markdown
   # [Supplier Name]
   
   **Status:** ✅ Selected & Approved
   **Selected For:** [Client] - [Date]
   **Product:** [What they provide]
   **Contact:** [Key contact info]
   
   [Complete info from template]
   ```

5. **Rename client folder:**
   ```bash
   mv "Clients/[Client Name]" "Clients/[Client Name] [ARCHIVED - Supplier Selected]"
   ```

6. **Commit to Git:**
   ```bash
   git add .
   git commit -m "Archive: Move [Supplier Name] to Suppliers folder - [Client] approved"
   git push
   ```

## 💡 Pro Tips

✅ **Document EVERYTHING** — You won't remember details 6 months later  
✅ **Get it in writing** — Email confirmations from client matter  
✅ **Be systematic** — Use matrices, don't rely on gut feeling  
✅ **Multiple suppliers** — Always have 2-3 options initially  
✅ **Verify references** — Actually call them, ask real questions  
✅ **Red flags** — Suppliers can't answer basic questions? Move on  
✅ **Use templates** — Save time, ensure consistency  
✅ **Regular commits** — Your git history is your audit trail
