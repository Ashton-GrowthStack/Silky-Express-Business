# BAYAG Quality Risk Assessment Framework

**Purpose:** Comprehensive supplier quality evaluation before client commitment

---

## PART 1: INFORMATION TO GATHER FROM SUPPLIER

### A. CERTIFICATIONS & COMPLIANCE
- [ ] **ISO Certifications** (ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001)
- [ ] **Product Certifications:**
  - ASTM F3404-23a (Polyurethane tile/flooring)
  - UFGS 09 66 23 (Federal resinous terrazzo)
  - EN standards (if selling to Europe)
  - SABS certification (if selling to South Africa)
- [ ] **Testing Laboratory** - 3rd party or in-house?
- [ ] **Accreditation** - Which accreditation bodies?

### B. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
**From TDS/MSDS extract:**
- [ ] **Solids Content** (% by volume/weight)
- [ ] **Viscosity** (cP - centipoise)
- [ ] **Density** (kg/L)
- [ ] **Pot Life** (minutes at standard temp)
- [ ] **Cure Time** (full cure at what temperature/humidity?)
- [ ] **VOC Content** (Volatile Organic Compounds)
- [ ] **Color/Color Stability** (vs yellowing)
- [ ] **UV Resistance** (test method & results)

### C. PERFORMANCE TEST RESULTS
- [ ] **UV Resistance Testing** (ASTM G154 or equivalent)
  - Hours exposed to UV
  - Yellowing measurement (ΔE value)
  - Gloss retention %
- [ ] **Water Absorption** (ASTM D570)
  - % weight gain after 24hr immersion
- [ ] **Abrasion Resistance** (ASTM D4157)
  - Haze/gloss loss after testing
- [ ] **Impact Resistance** (ASTM D2794)
  - Forward/reverse impact values
- [ ] **Adhesion Testing** (ASTM D3359)
  - Cross-hatch adhesion rating
- [ ] **Chemical Resistance** (acids, bases, salts)
  - Test substances & results

### D. MANUFACTURING & QUALITY CONTROL
- [ ] **Factory Location** - Where specifically? (Changchun, Jilin?)
- [ ] **Factory Age** - How long operating? (They claim 12 years)
- [ ] **Production Capacity** - How many liters/month?
- [ ] **Quality Control Process** - What testing per batch?
- [ ] **Batch Testing Documentation** - Can they provide?
- [ ] **Traceability System** - Can trace each batch?

### E. REAL-WORLD REFERENCES
- [ ] **Customer List** - Names, countries, contact info
- [ ] **Installation Photos** - Before/after (wall & floor)
- [ ] **Long-term Case Studies** - 3+ year performance data
- [ ] **Problem/Failure Cases** - How do they handle issues?
- [ ] **Warranty Claims** - Any significant claims? How resolved?

### F. APPLICATION SUPPORT
- [ ] **Crew Training** - Do they provide training for mixing/application?
- [ ] **Technical Support** - On-call support during installation?
- [ ] **Troubleshooting Guide** - Solutions for common problems?
- [ ] **Application Instructions** - Detailed procedures with visuals?
- [ ] **Environmental Requirements** - Exact temperature/humidity specs?

### G. SUPPLY CHAIN RELIABILITY
- [ ] **MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity)** - What's the minimum?
- [ ] **Lead Time** - From order to delivery in South Africa
- [ ] **Consistency** - Can they guarantee same batch quality?
- [ ] **Shelf Life** - Confirmed 24 months? How stored?
- [ ] **Packaging** - Safe for long-distance shipping?
- [ ] **Insurance/Liability** - Who covers damage in transit?
