# BAYAG Resin Washed Stone - Performance Data Summary
**For Client Evaluation**

---

## Product Overview

**BAYAG Resin Washed Stone** is a two-component, high-strength polyurethane-based flooring system specifically designed for both interior and exterior applications. This site-mixed resin washed-stone (resin-bound gravel) system combines durability with decorative aesthetics.

### Suitable Applications
- Interior & exterior walls
- Floors, stairs, and parking garages
- Driveways and balconies
- Pool edges and landscape paving
- Traffic-bearing outdoor areas

## Installation Requirements

### Substrate Preparation
- Must be sound, clean, and free of dust, grease, and contamination
- Must be dry before application
- Do NOT apply in rainy weather

### Post-Installation
- No foot traffic for 12 hours after installation
- Vehicle access permitted after 72 hours
- No water addition during mixing
- Ensure thorough mixing of resin and aggregate

---

## Storage & Shelf Life

- **Storage Temperature**: 5-35°C in dry, ventilated warehouse
- **Shelf Life**: 24 months from production date
- **Note**: Keep away from moisture; water contact impairs curing
