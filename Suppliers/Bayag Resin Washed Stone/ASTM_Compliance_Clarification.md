# ASTM F3403/F3404 Compliance Clarification

## Executive Summary
BAYAG Resin Washed Stone does **NOT** fall under ASTM F3403 or F3404 specifications. These standards apply only to pre-fabricated flexible polyurethane sheet/tile flooring products, not site-mixed resin washed-stone (resin-bound gravel) paving systems.

---

## Regulatory Context

### ASTM F3403 & F3404 Standards
- **Scope**: Pre-fabricated flexible polyurethane sheet and tile flooring products
- **Application**: Factory-manufactured, pre-cut flooring materials
- **Not Applicable**: Site-mixed or site-laid aggregate paving systems

### BAYAG Product Category
- **Type**: Site-mixed resin washed-stone (resin-bound gravel) paving system
- **Installation**: On-site application with two-component mixing
- **Standards Alignment**: ETAG 026 (European Technical Assessment Guideline for resin-bound systems)
