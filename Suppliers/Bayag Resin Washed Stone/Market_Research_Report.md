## 1. QUALITY ASSESSMENT

### ✅ POLYURETHANE IS PREMIUM TIER
- **100% solids UV-resistant polyurethane = Premium to High-Performance**
- NOT baseline standard—it's a technical upgrade

### Advantages over Epoxy:
✅ Won't yellow (huge advantage over standard epoxy)
✅ Superior flexibility (tolerates substrate movement)
✅ Better heat resistance (120°C vs epoxy 65°C max)
✅ Better surface scratch resistance

### Trade-offs:
❌ More moisture-sensitive during application
❌ Requires drier substrate conditions
❌ Higher application complexity
❌ More expensive than epoxy

---

## 2. KNOWN PROBLEMS & ISSUES

### Application Challenges (Two-Component Floor Systems):
**🚨 CRITICAL:** Mixing errors cause cracking
- Requires weight-based measurement (not volume)
- Pot life: 2-30 minutes (very fast!)
- Labor-intensive: Crews of 5+ required
- Environmental sensitive: 
  - Temperature-dependent (above 30°C = shorter pot life)
  - Requires substrate moisture <75% RH
  - Must work in well-ventilated areas
  - Moisture contamination ruins entire batch

### Common Durability Issues:
- **Cracking:** From substrate movement or improper mixing
- **Air bubbles/voids:** Application technique failures
- **Tacky surfaces:** Incomplete cure from wrong ratios
- **Once cured properly:** More durable than cement alternatives
