## 5. INSTALLATION CONSIDERATIONS

### ⚠️ CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS

**Installation Quality is Paramount:**
Resin-bound success depends heavily on:
1. **Proper substrate preparation** — Must be sound, clean, dust-free
2. **Correct mixing ratio** — A:B = 1.2:1 by weight (quality control critical)
3. **Environmental conditions** — No installation in rain (pot life: 45-60 min @ 25°C)
4. **Compaction** — Proper troweling and consolidation
5. **Curing time** — 72 hours for full load-bearing (logistics important)

**Challenges:**
- ⚠️ Durban's humidity can affect curing if not managed
- ⚠️ Summer rainy season limits installation windows
- ⚠️ Requires trained installers (not common in South Africa)
- ⚠️ Limited local expertise means initial market education needed

**Mitigations:**
- Partner with established local installers (African Eco model)
- Provide comprehensive training & certification
- Create detailed installation guides (environmental controls)
- Establish technical support presence in South Africa

## 6. MAINTENANCE & LONGEVITY

### ✅ LOW MAINTENANCE ADVANTAGE

**Expected Lifespan:** 15-25 years when installed correctly

**Maintenance Requirements:**
- Occasional cleaning (summer quarterly recommended)
- Remove debris, leaves, moss
- Prevent algae buildup in humid climate
- No sealing required (unlike concrete)

**Common Issues & Mitigation:**

| Issue | Cause | Risk in SA | Mitigation |
|-------|-------|-----------|-----------|
| **UV Fading** | Poor quality resin (aromatic polyurethane) | LOW - BAYAG uses aliphatic ✅ | Specify UV-stable formulation |
| **Chalking** | UV degradation + humidity fluctuations | MODERATE | Regular cleaning, protective coatings |
| **Cracking** | Poor substrate or thermal stress | MODERATE | Proper substrate prep, stress relief |
| **Moss/Algae Growth** | High humidity, shade areas | MODERATE | Regular cleaning in humid season |
| **Water Pooling** | Poor drainage design | MODERATE-HIGH | Proper slope design (critical in Durban) |

**South Africa-Specific Risk:** Heavy rainfall in Durban (April 2022 floods) means **drainage design is critical** — must address standing water issues.
