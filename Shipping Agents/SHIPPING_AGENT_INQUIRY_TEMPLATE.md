## SECTION 4: DESTINATION COUNTRY - SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS

### For South Africa Imports:

**Tax & Duty Structure:**
- [ ] **Customs Duty:** 30% of CIF value
- [ ] **Anti-Dumping Duty:** 7-45% of CIF value *(varies by manufacturer)*
- [ ] **Environment Levy:** R2.30 per kg of product
- [ ] **VAT:** 15% of (CIF + Customs + Anti-Dumping + Environment Levy)

### Scoring
- **Green Light:** Agent meets all criteria + best price + strong references
- **Yellow Light:** Agent meets most criteria, pricing competitive, needs reference verification
- **Red Light:** Missing critical requirements or concerns about reliability
