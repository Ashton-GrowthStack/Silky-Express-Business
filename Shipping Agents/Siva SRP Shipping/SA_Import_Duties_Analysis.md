## SA Import Tax Structure (VERIFIED)

1. **Customs Duty:** 30% of FOB value
2. **Anti-Dumping Duty:** 7-45% of FOB (VARIES BY MANUFACTURER)
3. **Environment Levy:** R2.30 per kg
4. **VAT:** 15% on total (FOB + duties + taxes)
