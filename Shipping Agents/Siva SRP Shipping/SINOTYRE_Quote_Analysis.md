## Shipping Terms - KEY INFORMATION FOR SIVA

### Incoterm: **FOB QINGDAO**
- **Port of Origin:** Qingdao, China
- **Meaning:** Free on Board - Seller loads goods on vessel, buyer responsible for freight insurance and risk from that point
- **Buyer Responsibility:** Shipping, Insurance, Customs, Delivery
- **Seller Responsibility:** Costs to get goods to Qingdao port

### Payment Terms
- **30% DEPOSIT** - Due upon agreement
- **70% BALANCE** - Paid before loading at port

### Shipping Conditions
- ✅ **Partial Shipment:** ALLOWED
- 📦 **Wrapping:** NO PACK
- ⏱️ **Delivery Timeline:** Goods must be shipped within 2 months after produced
- 💾 **Storage Fees:** Storage cost charged from 3rd month onwards
- 🛡️ **Insurance:** COVERED BY BUYER (not included in price)
