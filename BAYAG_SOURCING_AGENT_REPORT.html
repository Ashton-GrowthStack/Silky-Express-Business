SECTION 6: KNOWN PROBLEMS WITH TWO-COMPONENT SYSTEMS

🚨 Application Challenges

Mixing Errors = Cracking

- Requires weight-based measurement (NOT volume) - Pot life: 2-30 minutes (EXTREMELY FAST) - Labor intensive: Minimum 5-person crews required - One mixing error ruins entire batch ($1,000+ loss)

Environmental Sensitivity

- Temperature >30°C = shortened pot life - Substrate moisture must be <75% RH - Requires well-ventilated area - Moisture contamination = failed application

Common Durability Issues

- ❌ Cracking from substrate movement - ❌ Air bubbles/voids from poor application - ❌ Tacky/uncured surfaces from wrong mixing ratios - ✅ Once cured properly: Superior to epoxy alternatives ---
